About me

I have built roads and bridges now I build apps

For ten years I worked in civil engineering worker (construction worker) at Skanska and BRA Group on projects including the new Hisingsbron, retaining roads, landscaping and schools for the municipality. The job taught me to measure carefully, hold deadlines and stand behind what I hand over.

Since 2025 I have been on study leave, training as a Mobile Application Developer at Folkuniversitetet. I build native for both iOS and Android, and work a lot with AI tools on the side — including my own tool that extracts the design system out of a codebase.
